# **About Me**

<br>
<font size="+1">
Bridging the gap between sci-fi imagination and engineering reality, my work lives at the intersection of software, electronics, and control systems. I am driven by the challenge of transforming complex theory into autonomous machines that bring intelligence to the physical world—pushing the boundaries of technology to where no one has gone before.
<br><br>
My journey began at Istanbul Technical University, where a fascination with embedded systems course evolved into a bachelor’s thesis on quadrotor control. This sparked a career dedicated to robotics, leading me through the industry to work on the perception and navigation systems of autonomous warehouse robots. These roles gave me the hands-on grit required to build systems that don't just work in a lab, but thrive in the real world.
<br><br>
Now based in Canada, I am focused on deepening my technical stack and exploring the rigors of mission-critical systems. Recently, I have strengthened my foundation through The Construct’s Robotics Masterclass, specializing in ROS2 and modern C++. Whether I’m contributing to open-source initiatives or engineering real-time software, I am committed to advancing the reach and reliability of intelligent machines.
<br><br>
Welcome to my portfolio—a record of my journey in exploration and creation.
</font>
